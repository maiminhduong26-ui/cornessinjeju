01 agriculture

Select Fresh Cobs

Summer–Autumn harvest

Collected directly after the kernels are removed in Jeju's farming communities. Only firm, high-lignin cobs are selected to ensure strong fiber bonding.

Collection window • to be determined Selection rate: 78%
02 wb_sunny

Natural Sun Drying

Sun-dried on bamboo racks

Cobs are spread across natural bamboo racks and dried in Jeju sunshine for 5–7 days. Moisture is reduced to 12% or below to help prevent mold.

Moisture target • to be tested 57,350 solar-powered
03 grain

Bio-Fiber Milling

Micron-scale fiber processing

Mechanical milling breaks corn fibers into uniform particles. They are blended with a non-toxic, plant-based binder made from locally sourced organic cassava starch.

Particle size: 1.2–2.5 mm Natural binder
04 compress

Bio-Thermal Pressing

180-ton hydraulic pressure

Test formulations and pressing conditions experimentally. Water resistance, impact performance and heat tolerance are not yet established.

Press settings • to be determined experimentally Performance to be tested
05 palette

Heritage Engraving

Ceramic inlays & laser engraving

Handcrafted ceramic pieces featuring lotus or water motifs are inlaid into the surface, or the words “XUAN PHU COMMUNE” are laser-engraved as a mark of origin.

Integrated traceability QR code Durability • to be tested

LOCAL KNOWLEDGE WILL SHAPE THE JEJU PILOT

Local stories and quotations will be added only with permission after interviews with Jeju farmers, processors and workshop hosts.

CORNESS is more than a materials-recycling project; it is a livelihood initiative. In Jeju, over 45 women and older residents take part in fiber sorting and raw-material preparation, earning an average additional income of VND 4,200,000 per harvest for each participating family.

Every coaster carries the dedication of three generations of people whose lives are rooted in the red basalt soil of Xuan Loc.
