Local Travel & Experience Guide

Jeju's Featured Destinations (06 Places)

Explore Jeju’s volcanic coast, island heritage and citrus-growing traditions.

✦ 06 places to discover
01 / 06 Visit Jeju ↗

01 · UNESCO NATURAL HERITAGE

Seongsan Ilchulbong (Sunrise Peak)

Volcanic Tuff ConeCoastal Views

A dramatic volcanic tuff cone on Jeju’s eastern coast, known for its broad crater rim and ocean panoramas. Follow the marked visitor path and enjoy the view across the water.

✦Experience: The summit walk uses stairs; check current access and conditions before setting out.

◈Best for: Coastal scenery, geology and an iconic Jeju sunrise.

02 / 06 Visit Jeju ↗

02 · ISLAND HIGH POINT

Hallasan National Park

Mountain TrailsVolcanic Landscape

Hallasan rises at the center of Jeju and is South Korea’s highest mountain. Its park has several official hiking routes through changing forest and volcanic scenery.

✦Plan ahead: Trail access, reservations and weather conditions can change; confirm current guidance with the park.

◈Best for: Hikers prepared for a full day outdoors.

03 / 06 Visit Jeju ↗

03 · EAST COAST WALK

Seopjikoji

Coastal TrailVolcanic Rock

A coastal cape on eastern Jeju with open grassland, dark volcanic rock and wide sea views. The walking paths make this a relaxed stop between nearby coastal sights.

✦Setting: Exposed coastal paths can be windy; wear suitable shoes and check local conditions.

◈Best for: Easy coastal walks and landscape photography.

04 / 06 Visit Jeju ↗

04 · LIVING HERITAGE

Jeju Folk Village

Traditional HomesIsland Culture

An open-air heritage site bringing together traditional Jeju homes, everyday objects and craft traditions. Explore the village to learn how island life adapted to its landscape.

✦Explore: Check the venue’s current opening details and program before visiting.

◈Best for: Families and visitors interested in Jeju history and crafts.

05 / 06 Visit Jeju ↗

05 · CITRUS HERITAGE

Seogwipo Citrus Museum

Jeju TangerinesAgricultural Story

Discover the island’s citrus-growing heritage through displays on Jeju tangerines, varieties and cultivation, with visitor experiences connected to local citrus.

✦Address: 441 Hyodonsunhwan-ro, Seogwipo-si. Confirm current visitor services before traveling.

◈Best for: Food culture, citrus farms and family visits.

06 / 06 Visit Jeju ↗

06 · ORCHARD EXPERIENCE

Tangerine Field No. 76

Seasonal OrchardLocal Produce

A Jeju orchard experience centered on the island’s signature tangerines. Harvest activities are seasonal, giving visitors a closer look at local fruit-growing.

✦Season: Picking availability depends on the season and farm schedule; check with the orchard before visiting.

◈Best for: Visitors looking for a hands-on local food experience.
